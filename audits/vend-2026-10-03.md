## Found and fixed - the generated OpenAPI told a buyer nothing about `/api/v1/select`

The branch's own new law `test_generated_openapi_carries_input_params` **failed** on this
repository. `endpoint_meta.INPUT_SPECS` had no `/api/v1/select` entry, and
`complete_openapi` generates an operation for every paid manifest path from that table, so
`/api/v1/select` went out with `parameters: []`:

```
AssertionError: assert {'selector', 'url'} <= set()
```

The endpoint is sold - it is one of the 23 - and `server.py:2669` declares
`url`, `selector`, `attr` and `limit`. So a buyer reading `/openapi.json` to decide what to
send could not tell that the call it is about to **pay for** needs `url` and `selector`; it
would pay and then get a 400. This is the cherry-pick's gap, not a defect in the idea: #3
says it was taken from the server's `dcc359d`, which has a separate history, and the
server's 252-passing run is evidence about the server's tree rather than this one. The PR's
own caveat about not re-running the suite is exactly what hid it.

Fixed by adding the `/api/v1/select` entry to `INPUT_SPECS`, transcribed from the route's own
`Query` declarations, with `url` and `selector` required. The generated operation now carries
`[('url', True), ('selector', True), ('attr', False), ('limit', False)]` and the law passes.
