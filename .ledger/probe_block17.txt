Probe scenario (block 17-18, ARD/AI-Catalog rediscovery + call contract).

One end-to-end path through distinct components, from outside the box:

1. An outside agent-discovery crawler (the same behaviour the access log recorded on
   2026-09-17, when it got 404) resolves https://extract.paypercall.dev/.well-known/ard.json
   over public TLS and finds six ARD entries, each with a domain-anchored
   urn:air:paypercall.dev URN, displayName, media type, exactly one of url/data and
   2-5 representativeQueries, and each naming the Nano rail (nano:mainnet) and its price
   in metadata. The same document answers on /.well-known/ai-catalog.json.
2. The spec's own conformance tool (a second, independent implementation, fetched from
   ards-project/ard-spec and run locally) resolves the publisher path and reports
   CONFORMANCE STATUS: PASS with 0 errors.
3. The same entry source resolves on every endpoint subdomain the site serves, and
   robots.txt announces it with an Agentmap directive while the landing page emits rel="ard".
4. The crawler then reads /agents.txt and learns the call contract: the six endpoints with
   their exact prices, the Nano payout account, the X-PAYMENT header and the manifest URLs.
5. Finally the crawler calls one of the advertised endpoints unpaid and receives the
   x402 v2 402 challenge whose amount equals the price the ARD entry published — so the
   discovery document and the paywall agree, which is the thing a buyer's agent acts on.
   The x402 manifest is also reachable under both names it might try (/.well-known/x402 and
   /.well-known/x402.json), serving identical bytes.
