## What was built

### Prepaid Balance for Nano x402 calls

**Problem**: 14K+ visitors, 1 payer. Every call needs an on-chain transaction,
which means ~1 second confirmation delay, wallet interaction, and mental overhead
for repeat callers. The per-call friction kills adoption.

**Solution**: A buyer sends XNO to the treasury once (a "top-up"), and subsequent
calls draw from the prepaid balance via a simple `X-BALANCE: nano_...` header.
No on-chain interaction needed per-call — instant, zero friction.

**Implementation**:

1. **store.py** — Two new tables:
   - `balances`: account (PK), balance_raw, total_topup_raw
   - `balance_topups`: id, account, amount_raw, block_hash (UNIQUE), created_at
   - Functions: `top_up()`, `balance_of()`, `deduct_balance()`, `get_topup_history()`, `get_all_balances()`
   - All thread-safe through the existing module-level lock + BEGIN IMMEDIATE

2. **server.py** — Payment flow change:
   - `extract_balance_account()` reads and validates X-BALANCE header format
   - In `require_payment()`: if no X-PAYMENT but X-BALANCE is present with
     sufficient funds, deduct from balance instead of requiring on-chain block.
     This sits BEFORE the 402 response, so balance users never see a paywall.
   - `paid_response()` skips `store.resolve()` for balance calls (no redemption
     row), reports `X-Balance-Remaining` and `payment.balance_remaining_xno`.

3. **New endpoints**:
   - `POST /api/v1/balance/top-up` — Accept X-PAYMENT block, verify on-chain,
     credit full amount to sender's balance. Replay-protected via UNIQUE on block_hash.
   - `GET /api/v1/balance` — Check balance by ?account= or X-BALANCE header
   - `GET /api/v1/balance/topups` — List top-up history
   - `GET /api/v1/admin/balances` — (local-only) all accounts with balances

4. **Discovery manifests** updated: x402 manifest, agent-tools.json, agent-card.json
   all list the balance/top-up endpoints.

5. **402 message** updated to mention X-BALANCE as an alternative to X-PAYMENT.
